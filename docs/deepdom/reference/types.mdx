The drawing interfaces are in [Drawing interfaces](/reference/rendering). `ColorRef`, `VolCustomAttribute`, `ChartAreaRef` and the enums of the drawing are the same as in Deepchart: see [Attributes and types](/reference/attributes-and-types).

## CallHandler

`VolBookAPI.ExternalStructure.CallHandler`, the value of the `*Call` properties of `IndPrms`.

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `Off` | never called |
| `RT` | realtime only |
| `HistRT` | history and realtime |
| `Full` | `OnTickCall` only: `HistRT` plus `OnIniParallelTick` |

## BookBar

One bar of the chart. See [Bars and trades](/deepdom/concepts/bars-and-trades).

| Member | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `ExDt`, `LocDt` | time of the bar: exchange time, local time |
| `AskPrice`, `BidPrice` | best ask and bid at the end of the bar |
| `MaxAskPrice`, `MinBidPrice` | extremes of the bar |
| `AskVol`, `BidVol` | volume traded at the ask and at the bid |
| `IniTickBase`, `EndTickBase` | indices in `TickList` of the first and the last trade of the bar, `null` without trades |

## TickByTick

A trade, in `VolumetricaAPI.Connection.Structure` (alias `Feed` in the examples).

| Member | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `price`, `PinT` | price, as a number and as integer ticks |
| `Vol` | size |
| `bid`, `ask` | best bid and ask at the time of the trade |
| `exDt` | exchange time |

## AggrInfo

An aggregated trade, given to `OnTick` with the trade which completes it.

| Member | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `PinT` | price |
| `TradeDir` | `Ask`, `Bid`, `AboveAsk`, `BelowBid`, `Unknown` (`TradeDirStatus`) |
| `Vol` | total size |

## BookInfo

One level of the order book, in `VolumetricaAPI.Connection.Structure`.

| Member | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `PinT`, `Price` | the price |
| `isAsk` | the side |
| `Qty` | quantity at that price now; zero when the level is gone |
| `DiffQty` | change since the previous update |

## ProfileClass and VbpClass

The volume profile of the session (`Vbk.DailyProfile`).

| Member of `ProfileClass` | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `vbpItems` | the levels (`VbpClass`), indexed by `PinT`. `Values` to enumerate them, `SortedLHValues` from the lowest price up |
| `HinT`, `LinT` | highest and lowest price of the profile, as ticks |
| `LastPrice` | last price |

| Member of `VbpClass` | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `PriceInTicksLow`, `PriceInTicksHigh` | the price of the level, as ticks |
| `TotVol`, `AskVol`, `BidVol` | volume traded, and its ask and bid parts |
| `TotNT`, `AskNT`, `BidNT` | number of trades |

## LiquiditySumHelper

Sums of the first levels of each side of the book, see [Order book](/deepdom/concepts/order-book#liquiditysumhelper).

## Enums of the colors

In `VolBookAPI.Structure`, for `IMethodAPI.GetColorRefForTheme`.

| Enum | Values |
|---|---|
| `ColorReferenceEnum` | `Volume`, `Bid`, `Ask`, `Neutral`, `Up`, `Down`, `Container`, `Border` |
| `ColorTypeEnum` | `Stroke`, `Fill`, `Text` |
| `ColorRangeEnum` | `Default`, `Light`, `Strong`, `Range1` ... `Range4`, `Secondary` |

## Attributes of the parameters

| Attribute | Namespace | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `[Category]`, `[DisplayName]` | `System.ComponentModel` | section and label of a parameter |
| `[VolCustom]` | `VolumetricaControls` | order and limits, see [Parameters](/deepdom/concepts/parameters-and-colors#parameters) |
